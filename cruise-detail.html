7-Night Itinerary

  • D1

    Seattle, Washington

    Embark ms Meridian Aurora. Sail through Puget Sound as the Seattle skyline fades behind you.

  • D2

    Day at Sea

    Enjoy onboard amenities, cooking demonstrations, and lectures by Glacier Bay rangers.

  • D3

    Juneau, Alaska

    Alaska's capital and one of its most scenic cities. Visit Mendenhall Glacier or ride the Mount Roberts Tramway.

  • D4

    Skagway, Alaska

    Gateway to the Klondike Gold Rush. Ride the White Pass & Yukon Route railway into Canada.

  • D5

    Glacier Bay National Park

    UNESCO World Heritage Site. Witness towering tidewater glaciers calving into the sea.

  • D6

    Ketchikan, Alaska

    The "Salmon Capital of the World." Explore Creek Street, totems, and rainforest excursions.

  • D7

    Day at Sea

    Relax and reminisce on your journey through the Inside Passage.

  • D8

    Seattle, Washington

    Disembark in Seattle. Your Alaskan adventure concludes.
